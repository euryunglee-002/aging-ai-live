A network forecasts Boston's electricity demand every hour. Each of its 25 neurons drives one copper cell; when copper bridges a cell, that neuron is gone for good.

Each working neuron becomes current in its own cell

The same 25 cells seen from the side: a cathode hangs from above, an anode rises from below, and copper grows from the cathode toward the anode. The Arduino Mega runs the cells one at a time, N00 to N24, each for its pulse time.
